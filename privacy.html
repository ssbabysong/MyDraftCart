隐私政策 · 加班夜记

生效日期：2026 年 10 月 2 日

「加班夜记」是一个记录加班夜晚的小工具。我们不收集、不上传、也看不到你的任何数据。

你的数据存在哪里

你记录的内容（每晚的花费、下班时间、burnout 和身体不适次数、备注、愿望清单、设置）只保存在你的设备上。

在 iPhone App 里，这些数据还会通过 Apple 的 iCloud 同步到你登录同一个 Apple ID 的其他设备。iCloud 中的数据属于你的 Apple 账户，由 Apple 依照其隐私政策保管，开发者无法访问。没有登录 iCloud 时，数据只存在这台设备上。

我们不收集的东西

没有账号注册，没有广告，没有第三方统计或追踪 SDK，不读取通讯录、位置、相册等任何系统权限内的数据。

通知

如果你打开「每晚提醒」，App 会在本机设置定时的本地通知。通知由系统在你的设备上触发，不经过任何服务器。

导出与分享

只有当你主动点「导出备份」或「分享」时，App 才会生成文件，并交给系统分享菜单，由你决定发到哪里。

网络请求

App 和网页版会从 Google Fonts 加载手写字体文件。这类请求只下载字体，不包含你的任何记录。

删除数据

删除 App 即删除本机数据。要删除 iCloud 中的副本，请在 iPhone「设置 → Apple 账户 → iCloud → 管理账户储存空间」中删除「加班夜记」的数据。

儿童

本 App 不面向 13 岁以下儿童，也不收集任何人的个人信息。

联系我们

如有问题，请在 GitHub Issues 留言。政策如有更新，会在本页面公布。

Privacy Policy · Overtime Night Log

Effective October 2, 2026

Overtime Night Log is a small tool for logging late nights at work. We don't collect, upload, or have access to any of your data.

Where your data lives

Everything you log (spending, finish times, burnout and unwell counts, notes, wishlist and settings) is stored only on your device. In the iPhone app it also syncs through Apple's iCloud to your other devices signed in with the same Apple ID. Data in iCloud belongs to your Apple account and is handled by Apple under its privacy policy; the developer cannot access it.

What we don't collect

No accounts, no ads, no third-party analytics or tracking SDKs. The app doesn't read contacts, location, photos or any other personal data.

Notifications

If you turn on the nightly reminder, the app schedules local notifications on your device. No server is involved.

Export and sharing

Files are only created when you tap Export backup or Share, and they go to the system share sheet so you decide where they go.

Network requests

The app and website load handwriting fonts from Google Fonts. These requests only download fonts and never include your records.

Deleting your data

Deleting the app deletes the data on that device. To delete the iCloud copy, go to Settings → Apple Account → iCloud → Manage Account Storage on your iPhone and delete Overtime Night Log's data.

Contact

Questions? Open an issue at GitHub Issues. Any updates to this policy will be posted on this page.
