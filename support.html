常见问题

换手机了，记录怎么找回？
在新手机上登录同一个 Apple ID 并打开 iCloud，安装「加班夜记」后打开，记录会自动同步回来。

每晚提醒没响？
到 iPhone「设置 → 通知 → 加班夜记」确认通知已打开，再在 App「记录 → 设置 → 每晚提醒」里重新打开一次。

怎么换货币？
「记录 → 设置 → 货币」。记录统一按美元保存，换货币和改汇率只影响显示，不会改动数据。

桌面小组件怎么加？
长按桌面空白处 → 左上角「+」→ 搜索「加班夜记」→ 选择小号或中号。

怎么备份到别的地方？
「记录 → 备份 → 导出备份」，在分享菜单里选择存储位置。

FAQ

How do I get my records on a new phone?
Sign in with the same Apple ID and turn on iCloud, then install and open the app. Your records sync back automatically.

The nightly reminder doesn't show up.
Check Settings → Notifications → Overtime Night Log on your iPhone, then turn the reminder off and on again under Log → Settings in the app.

How do I change the currency?
Log → Settings → Currency. Records are stored in US dollars, so changing the currency or rate only changes how amounts are shown.

How do I add the widget?
Touch and hold the Home Screen, tap +, search for Overtime Night Log and pick the small or medium size.

How do I back up elsewhere?
Log → Backup → Export backup, then choose where to save it in the share sheet.
